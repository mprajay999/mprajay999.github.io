About

I moved from Hyderabad to Tampa on a hunch — that the same instinct for hunting down anomalies in a spreadsheet would translate into building software. It held up. I spent two years at Deloitte building backend services and automating audit workflows for a platform used by 1,000+ auditors — before starting an M.S. in Business Analytics & Information Systems at the University of South Florida (ended up TA-ing deep learning to a class of my own along the way). Today I'm a software developer at GroundGame.Health, building the messaging and compliance infrastructure that reaches health plan members at scale. Two domains — fintech audit and health-tech — but the same habit every time: take a messy manual process, build the pipeline and the product around it, and ship something people actually use.
