About

I started where correctness is non-negotiable. At Deloitte I built backend services for an investment-fund audit platform, where every price and holding had to reconcile against Bloomberg and Refinitiv and every discrepancy had to be explainable to an auditor. That work taught me the discipline most AI demos skip.

Then I went all-in on LLMs. As founding engineer at Ragnova, I built a multi-agent system on the Claude API that interviews a business owner and ships a live website in under five minutes. I did that while finishing an M.S. in Artificial Intelligence at USF and researching differentially private LLM fine-tuning.

Today I bring both halves together. At GroundGame.Health I ship production AI in a regulated industry: a compliance agent that audits 100% of member calls and texts with cited evidence, a RAG assistant that resolves 65% of benefits questions without a human, and voice agents that verify identity and hand off to live staff. All of it runs on a messaging platform I architected, which has processed 10M+ events.

In short, I can take an AI idea from prototype to a system a regulated business trusts with its members.
